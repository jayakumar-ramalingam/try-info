Consultancy

Evidence of technical advisory and transformation delivery.

Home Depot

Eleven continuous years of supplier-side consultancy through TCS and Mastech Digital across financial services, retail, and automotive technology. The Home Depot engagement supported an international retail organisation operating across the United States, Canada, and Mexico.

Modernisation approach

Developed specialist knowledge of Home Depot's merchandising lifecycle while working offshore from 2012 to 2015. After moving onsite, I advised merchant, product and engineering stakeholders on phased decomposition, cloud migration, event-driven pricing and controlled delivery.

Manheim, Cox Automotive

Applied the same advisory and delivery approach to high-throughput vehicle-data services, with scope later extended to vehicle-report generation.

How I Contribute

Independent technical judgment inside cross-functional work

I work with product, engineering, architecture, data, and operations stakeholders to clarify trade-offs and translate technical ambition into maintainable systems. At Home Depot, I provided technical delivery leadership to an onsite and offshore engineering group of more than twenty people, while business priorities and final approvals remained with Home Depot stakeholders.

Recommendations are grounded in scalability, failure modes, delivery sequencing, cost, security, observability, and long-term service ownership.

  • Item onboarding and SKU lifecycle
  • Assortment and planogram integration
  • Inventory, markdown and competitor-data services
  • Cloud migration and monolith decomposition
  • Event-driven pricing and store execution
  • High-throughput automotive vehicle-data services
  • Phased transformation and delivery governance

Demonstrated Outcomes

Transformation results across both engagements.

At Home Depot, the programme established integrated merchandising capabilities spanning item and SKU maintenance, pricing and store execution. Online prices could be updated in real time and store label-print requests dispatched on demand. Changes that previously required more than a week could reach customers in minutes. Improved pricing, markdown and waste visibility contributed to savings measured in millions.

At Manheim, successful delivery of the vehicle-data work resulted in my remit being extended to vehicle-report generation.

These internal outcomes and my personal contribution are supporter-verifiable. Confidential client records are not reproduced publicly.
